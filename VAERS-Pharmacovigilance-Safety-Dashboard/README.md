# VAERS Pharmacovigilance & Safety Dashboard

Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing VAERS vaccine safety reports, adverse events, patient demographics, vaccine characteristics, and serious outcomes.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

- **Pharmacovigilance & Safety Reports Overview** – Reporting trends, KPIs, serious vs. non-serious reports, and serious outcomes.
- **Vaccine Analysis** – Vaccines, manufacturers, vaccine types, dose series, routes, and vaccination sites.
- **Adverse Event & Symptom Analysis** – Top adverse events, symptom distribution, and serious vs. non-serious events.
- **Patient Demographics** – Age-group and sex distribution of reported cases.
- **Serious Outcome Analysis** – Hospitalization, disability, life-threatening events, death, and ER visits.

## 🛠️ Tools

**Power BI | Power Query | DAX | Data Modeling | Data Visualization**

## 🔍 Key Features

- Interactive slicers and cross-filtering
- KPI-based safety overview
- Distinct VAERS report analysis
- Adverse event and symptom analysis
- Vaccine and manufacturer analysis
- Demographic and serious-outcome analysis

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

VAERS is a spontaneous reporting system. Reported events do not necessarily indicate that a vaccine caused the event. This dashboard describes reporting patterns and should not be interpreted as establishing causality or incidence.
